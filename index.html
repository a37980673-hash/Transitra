The Vision

Bridging the Gap in Public Transport

Transitra isn't just another tracking app; it's a unified ecosystem. We noticed that while city metros have tracking, rural commuters and private bus users are left in the dark.

We connect passengers, drivers, and fleet owners through a single, lightweight platform optimized for even the lowest internet speeds.

Unified Rural & Urban Tracking

The Challenges We Solve

Unreliable Timing

Commuters wait hours without knowing if the next bus is coming or has already passed.

Zero Visibility

No live tracking means zero control over travel planning and increased frustration.

Rural Neglect

Villages are often left out of modern digital transport infrastructure mapping.

Private Fleet Gap

Private buses, the backbone of many regions, are rarely integrated into tracking systems.

How Transitra Works

A simple 4-step ecosystem for everyone.

1

Owner Registration

Bus owners register their fleet and assign unique IDs to each vehicle.

2

Trip Initiation

The Driver/Conductor starts the trip on the app selecting the designated route.

3

Live Telemetry

The device sends GPS data securely to our cloud at optimized intervals.

4

User Tracking

Commuters track the bus on a map with real-time ETA updates.

Powerful Features

Built for efficiency, safety, and scale.

Live GPS Tracking

Pinpoint accuracy for both city streets and winding rural roads.

Smart Route Search

Search via stops, bus names, or destination to find the best route instantly.

Multi-User Dashboard
